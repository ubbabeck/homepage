About

I'm a developer focused on protocol development and layer 2 solutions for bitcoin. My work centers on building secure, decentralized systems that uphold Bitcoin's core principles of trustlessness and permissionless access.


With expertise in Python, Golang, Rust, Flutter, and NixOS, I contribute to the Bitcoin ecosystem through protocol engineering and Lightning Network implementations. I'm particularly interested in improving Bitcoin security infrastructure and advancing layer 2 innovations.


My approach to development prioritizes long-term sustainability and accessibility, ensuring that the systems I build can be used by anyone, anywhere, without compromise on security.
